# Milestone 3 Balance Matrix

Validated against 30 complete baseline sessions and 12 complete post-tune sessions. All sessions used the real Phaser scene, seeded game rules, automatic combat, natural movement/dash input, natural perk offers, all events, and the full Final Escalation.

## Characters

| Character | Baseline (15 sessions) | Post-tune (6 sessions) | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Firestarter | 15 wins; 20.73 average damage; 362,174 score; 721 coins; 728 clears | 6 wins; 15.00 damage; 396,265 score; 777 coins; 748 clears | Viable with every strategy; offensive identity produces the higher score/clear ceiling. |
| Cool Head | 15 wins; 25.07 average damage; 347,181 score; 928 coins; 723 clears | 6 wins; 24.83 damage; 359,248 score; 979 coins; 725 clears | Viable with every strategy; wider collection and rechargeable boundary produce a clear coin/safety tradeoff. |

## Corporate events and boss

All five events appeared in both accepted cohorts. In post-tune validation, each appeared in seven or eight of 12 sessions; every associated run remained winnable, with average total damage between 17.13 and 21.13. This provides changing pressure without an unavoidable failure event.

Both characters reached and defeated all four boss phases in every accepted session (42/42). Aggressive, balanced, and defensive builds all won without requiring a specific perk.
